OmniDeck third-party components
===============================

FFmpeg
------
OmniDeck bundles an FFmpeg Windows build from BtbN/FFmpeg-Builds. The selected build
is GPL-licensed. FFmpeg source and licensing information are available at:
https://github.com/BtbN/FFmpeg-Builds
https://ffmpeg.org/legal.html

ViGEmBus
--------
The optional virtual gamepad driver is ViGEmBus 1.22.0 by Nefarius Software
Solutions e.U. The driver is licensed under BSD-3-Clause. The project is retired
and no longer receives updates. Upstream project and license:
https://github.com/nefarius/ViGEmBus
https://github.com/nefarius/ViGEmBus/blob/master/LICENSE
